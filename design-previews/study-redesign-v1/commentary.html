READING · REV 12:6 (KJV)“And the woman fled into the wilderness, where she hath a place prepared of God…”
📚 Uriah Smith · Daniel & the Revelation Verse 6
The wilderness here denotes the retired, solitary condition of the church during the 1260 years of papal rule — hidden, yet prepared of God, nourished “a time, and times, and half a time.” Compare Dan 7:25.

When heaven seems silent, God is preparing a place. The woman does not manufacture the wilderness — she flees into what God prepared. Obedience first, provision follows.
