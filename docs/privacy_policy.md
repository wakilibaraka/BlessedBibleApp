# Privacy Policy

**The Blessed Bible** · Effective date: October 10, 2026

## Summary

The Blessed Bible works fully offline and does not require an account. Your reading, bookmarks, highlights, notes and settings stay on your device unless you choose to sign in or export a backup.

There are no ads and no analytics, and we never sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Who we are

The Blessed Bible is developed by Emmanuel Baraka, who is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

## Information stored on your device

Your reading position, bookmarks, folders, highlights, notes, reading plans and progress, reading streak, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data.

If you use Export backup, the app creates a file and hands it to the share destination you pick. Where that file goes is up to you.

## If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Google or Apple. Google Firebase Authentication then receives your name, email address (Apple may provide a private relay address) and an account identifier, which we use only to manage your account.

While signed in, reading plans you create are also saved to your account in Google Cloud Firestore so they can be restored. No other personal content is uploaded at this time.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics collects technical details: the error and where in the code it happened, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your Bible reading, notes or other personal content.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Send crash reports.

## App integrity checks

To protect our servers from abuse, the app uses Firebase App Check, which asks Google Play Integrity (Android) or Apple App Attest / DeviceCheck (iOS) to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. These services process device attestation data under Google's and Apple's own privacy policies.

## Downloading content

When you download a Bible translation, the app requests it from the Free Use Bible API (bible.helloao.org) or, for KJV with Strong's numbers, from GitHub. When the app refreshes study content, it reads from Google Cloud Firestore. As with any internet request, these servers receive your device's IP address. No account information is sent.

## Notifications and widgets

Reading reminders and home-screen widgets are scheduled and updated on your device. We do not use a push-notification server.

## Who we share data with

We use service providers only to run the features above: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Crashlytics, App Check, Play Integrity), Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Attest / DeviceCheck), and the Free Use Bible API and GitHub for translation downloads. We do not sell personal data or share it for marketing or advertising.

## How long we keep data

Account data and cloud copies of your reading plans are kept until you delete your account. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app.

## Deleting your account

In the app, go to the Study tab, tap your avatar at the top, then Account > Delete Account. This deletes your sign-in record and all data stored in your account in the cloud, and clears your personal data from that device. You can also email us to request deletion; we will complete it within 30 days.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR or Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data and to object to or restrict its processing. Use Export backup for a copy of your on-device data, Delete Account to erase your cloud data, or contact us for anything else. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Security

Data sent between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Cloud data is protected by access rules that let only your signed-in account read or change it.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Sign-in is optional; the app works fully without it.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and its effective date, and note significant changes in the app's release notes.
