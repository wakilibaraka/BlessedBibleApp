Delete your The Blessed Bible account

An account is optional. If you signed in with Google or Apple, you can delete it and all of its cloud data.

In the app

  1. Open the app, go to the Study tab and tap your avatar at the top.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete Account, and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email wakilibar@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for your account to be deleted. We complete requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your sign-in record and everything stored in your account in the cloud (reading plans and any synced data). Crash reports are not linked to your account and expire after 90 days. Data stored only on your device is removed when you delete it in the app or uninstall.
