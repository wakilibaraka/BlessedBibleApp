## 📖 Blessed Bible v1.0.7 Release Notes

Blessed Bible v1.0.7 turns the chapter player into a real listening experience. The reader follows the narrated verse and steps aside when you scroll, the World English Bible text ships with the app so read-along works offline on a fresh install, a verse-aware scrubber and a sleep timer handle the controls, and Home offers to resume the last chapter you played. Chapter downloads are now managed from **Settings → Storage** as well as the player, and a verse-sync correction is remembered per chapter, so a chapter only needs fixing once.

**App version:** `1.0.7` (`versionCode` 2014) · **Package:** `com.baraka.bibleapp` · **Minimum Android:** 8.0 (API 24)

### 🌟 What's New in v1.0.7

#### 🎧 Audio Bible

- **Read along:** the chapter text follows the narrated verse, steps aside the moment you scroll, and offers "Back to the playing verse".
- **Offline read-along from install:** the World English Bible text ships with the app (~5 MB), so a fresh install follows the narration without downloading a translation first.
- **Verse-aware scrubber:** one tick per verse — tap to jump to a verse or drag to scrub, with the current verse named above the bar.
- **Built for listening:** continuous play (next chapter, or stop at the end of the book), a 15/30/45/60-minute sleep timer, 10-second skips, and 0.75×–2× speed.
- **Keeps playing:** a mini player and media notification with lock-screen controls mean closing the player sheet or turning the screen off does not stop the chapter.
- **Resume from Home:** a **Continue listening** card opens the last chapter you played, at the position you left it.
- **Downloads live in Settings → Storage:** the chapter-audio cache shows its size and a clear action beside the app's other storage rows, instead of only inside the player sheet.
- **Corrections stick:** tapping the verse you hear re-syncs the chapter, and that correction is saved per chapter and restored the next time you open it — a listener only has to correct a chapter once.

#### 🔧 Under the hood

- Verse-sync anchors persist in `audio_sync_anchors` as `book`, `chapter`, `verse`, `position_ms` and `saved_at`, one per chapter, capped at 200 with the oldest dropped first. Unreadable entries are skipped rather than breaking read-along.
- Stored corrections load once at startup and are applied before a chapter's first timeline is built, so reopening a corrected chapter starts in sync instead of jumping a second later.
- 455 unit and widget tests (6 new) cover the anchor store: round-trip, per-chapter isolation, re-correction, cap eviction, corrupt-store recovery, and a restored anchor re-basing the timeline.

#### 📖 Everything from v0.0.7

Reader, reading plans, commentary cleanups, localization, account controls and the original streaming player are unchanged — see the [v0.0.7 notes](https://github.com/wakilibaraka/BlessedBibleApp/releases/tag/v0.0.7).
