## 📖 Blessed Bible v0.0.7 Release Notes

Blessed Bible v0.0.7 makes "Read in context" land in the chapter reader with the verse already highlighted, gives reading plans real completion dates and a streak, localizes the last English book name in the plan reader, cleans up how passage text is typeset in commentary and the Story of the Day, and adds a player that streams the public-domain World English Bible narration for the chapter you are reading.

### 🌟 What's New in v0.0.7

#### 📖 Reader
- **Read in context opens the chapter reader:** "Read in context" on the Verse of the Day, a commentary pull-quote, or a verse preview now opens the plan-style chapter reader at the named chapter with that verse accented and scrolled into view, instead of only switching to the Read tab.

#### ⚡ Performance
- **Home opens without freezing:** Verse of the Day is picked without reworking every commentary entry, the other tabs build just after Home instead of with it, and the offline Bible loads book by book so the splash keeps moving.

#### 🎓 Study
- **Full card text:** Study cards no longer cut off with "...". Verse of the Day shows the whole verse, and plan titles, readings and snippets show in full.
- **Word of the Day card removed:** It no longer appears on Study. The Dictionary and its Word of the Day history are unchanged.

#### 📅 Verse of the Day
- **No skipped or repeated verses:** The daily verse is picked by calendar day, not by elapsed time, so a clock change or a different time zone no longer makes the app skip a verse one day and show it twice later. Archive dates, tags and taps resolve to the same verse the card shows.

#### 📅 Reading Plans
- **Completion dates:** every finished day records the date it was read, so a plan now knows whether you read today and when you last did. Progress carried over from older builds stays readable, just undated.
- **Plan streak:** the plan page shows a streak of consecutive days with a completed reading. Today not being read yet does not break it, and a clock change across a DST boundary can no longer skip or repeat a day.
- **Pace changes keep their progress:** changing a custom plan's day count used to re-cut the schedule and leave the old progress keyed to the old days, so finished days could read as unread on the next launch. Progress is now re-keyed to the new schedule.
- **Honest catch-up:** "mark all previous as read" dates the days it completes, keeps dates that already existed, and never touches the day you are on.

The plan engine is covered by a new stress suite: a full 365-day plan is completed, persisted, reloaded and checked; 183 unchecks followed by 183 rechecks leave no drift; a year-long streak is counted; 20,000 unordered dates are processed; and a 365-day plan is re-paced to 90 days and back with no verse lost.

#### 🎧 Audio Bible
- **Listen to the chapter you are reading:** a headphones button in the reader streams the public-domain World English Bible narration for that chapter, about 0.2–1 MB each, instead of bundling gigabytes of audio. No narration files ship in the app, so the download stays small; listening needs a connection unless you download a chapter first.
- **Offline only when you ask:** download a single chapter to play it without a connection. Downloads are capped at 200 MB, the oldest are dropped first, and the player clears them on request.
- **Built for listening:** continuous play auto-advances into the next chapter, including across the end of a book, with 10-second skips and 0.75×–2× speed. Closing the player sheet does not stop playback.

#### 📚 Commentary
- **The card quotes its own verse:** A commentary card opened in chapter mode now shows the text of the verse its heading names, instead of the verse you happened to select — no more verse 27's wording under a "Daniel 11:26" label.
- **Quote cards keep their formatting:** Pull-quotes and the Story of the Day key verse render bold, underline, and tappable scripture references like the body text, instead of printing `**` and `__`.
- **Fewer broken words:** Words hyphenated across a print line break are rejoined, OCR sentences glued to the next word are spaced apart, and soft hyphens no longer reach the reader.
- **Cleaner scanned text:** The Uriah Smith volumes' `:--` / `,--` shorthand is now a real em dash, inline `[Illustration: …]` plate captions are gone, and leaked page numbers no longer split sentences. The 19 entries too corrupt for any rule are listed for re-import rather than half-cleaned.

#### 🌍 Localization
- **The plan card speaks your language:** The mark-done card in the plan reader localizes its book name, so Kiswahili shows "Mwanzo 1" instead of "Genesis 1".

#### 🔐 Account
- **Sign out to a guest:** One sheet asks whether to keep your study data and your name on this device (both ticked). You always land as a guest, under your name or "Guest".
- **Reset this device:** Erases study data on this device only. Signed in, choose to sign out or stay signed in and re-download. Deleting this app's cloud data is a separate switch, off by default, confirmed by typing DELETE.
- **Your name:** Guests can name themselves; signed-in name edits update the shared account (Blessed Arcade too).
- **No more blank screens:** Finishing or restarting onboarding no longer replaces the app's root screen, slow account actions show progress, and Delete account, Back up and Restore from the account menu work again.
